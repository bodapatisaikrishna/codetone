# Code of Conduct

CodeTone is a small project and we want it to stay a friendly place to work on
it.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and assume good faith.
- Give feedback on the work, not the person. Be specific and constructive.
- Accept feedback and disagreement gracefully, and explain technical objections
  with reasons.
- Respect the maintainer's time. Reviews are volunteer work.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, personal attacks, or discrimination of any kind.
- Trolling, deliberately derailing discussions, or spam.
- Publishing someone's private information without their permission.
- Any other conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This applies to all project spaces: issues, pull requests, discussions, and
comments, and to anyone representing the project in public.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, close or lock threads, and block
people who break these rules.

To report a problem, message the maintainer privately through their
[GitHub profile](https://github.com/bodapatisaikrishna), or open a minimal issue
asking for private contact without describing the details. Reports are handled
privately.

## Attribution

This is a short code of conduct written for this project. It follows the spirit
of the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/).
